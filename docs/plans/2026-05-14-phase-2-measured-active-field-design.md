# Phase 2 Measured Active Field Design

## Goal

Turn the current conceptual wall sketch into a measured `90 in x 80 in` active-field blueprint, then create the first mechanism dossiers for the assemblies that shape the whole composition.

Phase 2 is not the full creative mechanism-language phase. It defines the measured stage, names the major causal anchors, and protects the kitchen constraints before the mechanism library expands.

## Confirmed Field

The active field is `90 in` wide by `80 in` high.

This is conservative relative to the available wall area:

- From the right edge of the aprons/gloves to the outlet region is approximately `95 in`.
- From the outlet region up to the ceiling is approximately `86 in`.
- The extra margin around the active field exists, but it is caution territory rather than design territory.

The blueprint should treat the active field as the normal buildable canvas. Parts may protrude or briefly pass outside it only when the reason is explicit and the risk is understood.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not make the outlet inaccessible.
- Do not block or interfere with the door.
- Do not occupy the apron/glove rack area.
- Do not go past the pipe/rod boundary on the right.
- Treat the tiny top-left space above the door as overflow only.
- Treat the few inches below and beside the outlet as overflow only.

The right, lower, and top-left overflow areas are "playing with fire" zones. They may be annotated in the blueprint, but they should not become default mechanism space.

## Blueprint Direction

Update `design/wall-layout/active-field.svg` from a placeholder sketch into a measured active-field blueprint.

The SVG should continue to use inches as its coordinate system. It should label:

- `90 in x 80 in` active field.
- Left trigger column.
- Entangled mechanism field.
- Gong court.
- Outlet clearance and lower caution band.
- Right pipe boundary caution.
- Door and apron/glove caution.
- Top-left overflow caution.
- Depth zones for shallow, normal, and theatrical projection.
- Candidate routes as provisional causal paths, not final tracks.

The blueprint should preserve fantasy and changeability. It should not pretend that exact tracks, final gong positions, or final mechanism sizes are settled.

## First Mechanism Dossiers

Create dossiers only for mechanisms that define composition, safety, route logic, or major wall real estate. These dossiers are design anchors, not final engineering drawings.

Initial dossiers:

- `left-meal-trigger-column.md`
- `shared-launch-and-diverter.md`
- `breakfast-high-gong-strike.md`
- `lunch-medium-gong-strike.md`
- `dinner-low-gong-strike.md`
- `stored-energy-release.md`
- `marble-route-field.md`

This boundary keeps Phase 2 focused. Ideas like second domino languages, clever funnels, kitchen-tool levers, and unusual object jokes should be captured in the backlog or a mechanism pantry, then expanded intentionally in the next creative phase.

## Phase Boundary

Phase 2 answers:

- Where is the measured active field?
- What nearby wall areas are caution-only overflow?
- Which mechanisms are unavoidable enough to shape the whole wall?
- What safety, reset, mounting, and depth assumptions must be visible before physical proof-of-concepts begin?

The next phase should be a mechanism-language and dossier-expansion phase. That is where the project should deliberately invent and compare domino languages, funnels, sequencing devices, object-based jokes, and route identities.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The active field is documented as `90 in x 80 in`.
- The outlet, door, aprons/gloves, pipe boundary, lower band, and top-left overflow are documented as constraints or caution zones.
- `active-field.svg` shows the measured blueprint and no longer reads as a generic placeholder.
- The mechanism index links the first composition-defining dossiers.
- Each first dossier captures purpose, input, output, energy source, reset or arming action, mounting/depth assumptions, risks, and open tuning questions.
- The checker includes the Phase 2 files so the documentation foundation remains verifiable.
