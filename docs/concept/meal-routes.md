# Meal Routes

The route design is intentionally provisional. The machine should not look like three separate lanes.

## Breakfast

- Gong: high pitch.
- Route identity: fast, bright, upward-feeling, and likely carried by a light rolling-body signal.
- Provisional strike language: falling ball, rebound strike, or another crisp high-gong action.
- Constraint: should not become just a shorter copy of the lunch route.

## Lunch

- Gong: medium pitch.
- Route identity: balanced, switchable, and field-crossing.
- Provisional strike language: pendulum beater or another centered, legible release.
- Constraint: should show the shared field most clearly without feeling like the default route.

## Dinner

- Gong: low pitch.
- Route identity: slower, heavier, and more visibly armed.
- Provisional strike language: cocked mallet, gravity hammer, or another local stored-energy release.
- Constraint: must keep moving mass and stored energy safe, especially near the lower/right caution areas.

## Shared Field

The routes should share neighborhoods, cross, wake dormant mechanisms, and use repeated mechanism families in different languages.

These identities are not final tracks. They are starting constraints for future mechanism-language work.
