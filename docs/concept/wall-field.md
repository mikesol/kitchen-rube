## Active Field

The machine should not occupy the whole wall. The working design field is 90 in. wide by 80 in. high, padded away from the glove/apron area, pipes, outlet, floor, ceiling, and normal human movement through the kitchen.

Treat the active field as the measured buildable canvas. The tiny space above the door in the top-left, the lower space around and below the outlet, and the few inches near the right pipe boundary are overflow zones. They may be annotated, used for clearance, or briefly crossed by a justified moving element, but they should not host normal mechanism neighborhoods.

## No-Go And Caution Zones

- Do not block the outlet.
- Do not conflict with the pipe/rod area on the right.
- Do not interfere with the door, handle, or glove/apron rack.
- Do not rely on the top-left above-door area except as a caution overflow note.
- Do not use the few inches below or beside the outlet unless the outlet remains accessible and visually clear.
- Keep deeper protrusions high or toward the right/back of the kitchen.

## Mounting Intent

The installation should feel like it is on the wall, not like a cabinet in front of it. Use thin modular backer panels or slats with hidden reinforcement where needed.
