# Marble Clutch Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`, `clarity: needs-storyboard`

Suggested art format: `art: three-frame storyboard`

## Purpose

Temporarily couple a rolling ball to a rotating wheel, letting the ball's weight or momentum advance the wheel one partial turn before the ball exits.

## Subject Brief

Show a small vertical wheel mounted on a wall backer. The wheel has one open notch or pocket at its rim. In the armed state, the notch waits under a short marble chute.

Frame 1: the wheel is still, with the empty notch aligned under the incoming ball path. Frame 2: a marble drops into the notch. Its weight or impact turns the wheel a short distance. A peg on the wheel pushes a latch, flag, or gate as the wheel rotates. Frame 3: the notch reaches an exit opening and the marble falls out into a capture cup or next track. The wheel stops in a new indexed position or returns by spring/counterweight.

This is not a decorative wheel. The ball briefly becomes part of the wheel and makes the wheel perform work.

## Must Show

- Wheel pivot.
- One clear rim notch or pocket.
- Ball entering the notch.
- Wheel output peg or cam pushing a latch.
- Ball exit path.

## Avoid

- A marble merely rolling past a wheel.
- A wheel spinning freely with no stop.
- A pocket that traps the ball forever.

## Open Questions

- Does the wheel index one step or return after each use?
- How does the ball exit without jamming?
- Is gravity alone enough, or does the incoming ball need speed?
