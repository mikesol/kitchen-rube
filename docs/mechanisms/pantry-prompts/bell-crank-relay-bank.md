# Bell-Crank Relay Bank Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`, `clarity: needs-prior-art`

Suggested art format: `art: single-frame`

## Purpose

Transfer motion through a dense shared neighborhood by changing direction repeatedly with compact pivot levers.

## Subject Brief

Show a shallow wall-mounted cluster of L-shaped bell cranks. A horizontal push from the left hits the first crank. That crank turns the push into a vertical pull. The vertical pull moves a second crank, which turns it into a sideways latch release. A third crank can redirect motion again.

The point is a compact relay neighborhood: a route can pass through without needing a long straight track. Different cranks may be brass, wood, or small metal levers, but each must have a visible pivot and contact point.

## Must Show

- Several L-shaped pivot levers.
- Clear pivots.
- Input motion from one direction.
- Output motion in a different direction.
- Contact or linkage between cranks.

## Avoid

- Random levers with no input/output chain.
- Hidden linkages behind the board.
- Cranks that all rotate without transferring work.
