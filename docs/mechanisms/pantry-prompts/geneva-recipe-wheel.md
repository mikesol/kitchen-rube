# Geneva Recipe Wheel Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`, `clarity: needs-prior-art`

Suggested art format: `art: ghosted-motion`

## Purpose

Advance a visible program wheel exactly one station per event, using intermittent motion rather than free rotation.

## Subject Brief

Show a recipe-card wheel or menu wheel mounted vertically on the wall. Around the wheel are several radial slots. A separate driver arm has a pin that enters one slot briefly, turns the wheel one step, then exits. Between steps, a locking surface or detent holds the recipe wheel still.

The visual language can use recipe cards or meal marks, but the mechanics should be a Geneva-style intermittent indexer: driver pin enters slot, wheel advances one station, wheel locks again.

Use ghosted positions to show the wheel before and after one step.

## Must Show

- Slotted driven wheel.
- Driver pin entering a slot.
- One-step advancement.
- Locked dwell position between steps.
- Output mark or recipe card that changes after the step.

## Avoid

- A normal free-spinning wheel.
- Hidden gearing.
- Text-heavy recipe labels.

## Open Questions

- What input drives the pin: ball impact, lever stroke, or cord pull?
- How many stations are useful?
- Should this be informed by a prior-art Geneva mechanism before image generation?
