# Domino Gap Crossed By Pendulum Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`, `clarity: needs-storyboard`

Suggested art format: `art: three-frame storyboard`

## Purpose

Insert a visible timed pause into a falling-tab sequence by requiring a pendulum to bridge a deliberate gap.

## Subject Brief

Show a row of falling domino tabs that stops at a gap. Across the gap is a pendulum mounted above or beside the row. The first section of the row releases the pendulum or lets it begin swinging.

Frame 1: the first domino section has fallen and the sequence is stopped at the gap. The next tab is too far away to be touched directly. Frame 2: the pendulum swings through the gap after a delay. Frame 3: at the bottom or far side of its swing, the pendulum taps the next tab and restarts the falling sequence.

The pause should be mechanically visible: the chain waits because the pendulum has to travel, not because the image says "delay."

## Must Show

- Domino row interrupted by a clear gap.
- Pendulum pivot and swing arc.
- Pendulum physically contacting the next tab.
- Restart of the sequence after the delay.

## Avoid

- A continuous domino row with no real gap.
- Pendulum as decorative background.
- Contact geometry that misses the next tab.

## Open Questions

- What releases the pendulum?
- How long should the pause be?
- Does the pendulum need a catch after it bridges the gap?
