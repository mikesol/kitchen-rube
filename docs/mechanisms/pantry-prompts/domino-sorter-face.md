# Domino Sorter Face Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`

Suggested art format: `art: single-frame`

## Purpose

Encode route identity by physical height, so different incoming signals hit different tabs and open different downstream gates.

## Subject Brief

Show a front-facing panel with several upright tabs of different heights. Each tab has its own pivot and is linked to a different gate or cord. An incoming ball, lever, or sliding pusher arrives at a particular height.

A low incoming signal hits only the short tab. A mid-height signal clears the short tab and hits the middle tab. A high signal reaches the tall tab. Each struck tab tips or falls and opens a different route.

The mechanism should read like a physical decoder face: height in, route out.

## Must Show

- Three or more tabs with visibly different heights.
- Incoming signals at different heights.
- Different outputs linked to different tabs.
- Clear contact geometry.

## Avoid

- Tabs that all get hit by the same input.
- Decorative height differences with no output difference.
- Ambiguous routes after the tab is hit.

## Open Questions

- Is the incoming signal a ball, lever, or sliding bar?
- How much height separation is needed to be reliable?
- Does this belong near the selector or in the shared field?
