# Vertical Shutter Dominoes Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`

Suggested art format: `art: ghosted-motion`

## Purpose

Use falling hinged doors to reveal or unblock a path, so a sequence of tabs physically opens the next route rather than merely looking active.

## Subject Brief

Show a vertical bank of small hinged shutters on a wall-mounted backer. Each shutter covers a slot, hole, cord path, or short marble chute. The shutters are weighted tabs with small hinges on one side or along the top edge.

In the armed state, the shutters are closed, covering the path. A falling tab or ball opens the first shutter. Once open, that shutter exposes the next slot and allows a ball, cord, or latch finger to reach the next shutter. The sequence continues as doors fall open one by one.

The concept should read like a set of tiny mechanical cabinet doors or vents opening in sequence, but every opened shutter must unblock a real path.

## Must Show

- Hinges on each shutter.
- A blocked path behind the shutters.
- At least one shutter in closed position and one ghosted open position.
- A ball, cord, or latch that can only continue after a shutter opens.

## Avoid

- Decorative doors with no blocked path.
- Shutters floating without hinges.
- A path that would work even if the shutters were absent.

## Open Questions

- Should shutters hinge sideways like cabinet doors or downward like trapdoors?
- Is the payload a marble, cord, falling pin, or latch finger?
- How does reset close all shutters cleanly?
