# Falling Domino Decoder Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`, `clarity: needs-storyboard`

Suggested art format: `art: three-frame storyboard`

## Purpose

Make meal selection visible as a *choice* by showing three possible domino subchains and firing only the one the meal trigger picks. The unchosen subchains stay standing so the audience can read what did not happen.

## Subject Brief

Show three short domino subchains standing on a wall backer, side by side. Each subchain leads to the same downstream latch but takes a visibly different path to get there: one short and direct, one looping or zigzagging, one slow and stretched. The three paths should be distinguishable at a glance, because the *unfired* subchains are part of the picture.

Above the three subchain heads, a single horizontal selector arm hangs from a small pivot. The arm can slide sideways along a short track and can drop downward. In the armed state, the arm is raised and aligned with none of the heads.

Frame 1: all three subchains stand armed, the selector arm is raised and centered, the downstream latch is closed. Frame 2: the meal trigger shifts the selector arm sideways to align with one chosen head, then releases it. The arm drops, tipping only that head; the other two subchain heads remain standing. Frame 3: the chosen subchain has fallen along its distinctive path and tripped the downstream latch. The unchosen subchains are still upright, visibly armed but inert.

The key mechanical idea is that the selector arm physically reaches only one subchain at a time, so no logic is hidden behind the board. The selection is visible the instant the arm moves.

## Must Show

- Three subchains with visibly distinct paths.
- A single selector arm that can slide and drop.
- The arm aligned with exactly one head before release.
- One subchain falling and the other two staying upright.
- A shared downstream latch reached only by the chosen subchain.

## Avoid

- All three subchains falling at once.
- A branching pattern with no selective start.
- Hidden logic behind the board that decides the route.
- Three subchains that look identical, since the picture relies on their differences.

## Open Questions

- Should the selector arm be cocked by the meal trigger directly, or by a small intermediate mechanism that records the meal first?
- Do the unfired subchains reset by hand, or does a sliding reset comb stand all of them back up at once?
- Is the arm best as a sliding rail traveler, a pivoting pointer, or a small carriage on a short track?
