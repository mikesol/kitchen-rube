# Three-Latch Braid Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`, `clarity: needs-storyboard`

Suggested art format: `art: three-frame storyboard`

## Purpose

Make dinner feel ceremonially armed by requiring three visible latch releases in order before a final gate can open.

## Subject Brief

Show three wall-mounted latches arranged in a diagonal or braided path. Latch A blocks or protects latch B. Latch B blocks or protects latch C. Latch C blocks the final output gate.

Frame 1: all three latches are armed and the final gate is locked. Frame 2: the first route signal releases latch A, which exposes or enables latch B. Frame 3: after B and C have advanced, the final gate opens or a stored-energy release becomes available.

The braid should not be a literal rope braid unless that helps. It should read as a sequence of interdependent latches: A enables B, B enables C, C opens output.

## Must Show

- Three distinct latches.
- Dependency between latches.
- Final gate blocked until the sequence completes.
- Visible armed and spent states.

## Avoid

- Three independent latches that can fire in any order.
- Decorative braiding with no mechanical dependency.
- A final output that opens before all three latches release.

## Open Questions

- What resets the three latches cleanly?
- Is this a dinner-only mechanism or a general sequencing language?
- What physical input trips each latch?
