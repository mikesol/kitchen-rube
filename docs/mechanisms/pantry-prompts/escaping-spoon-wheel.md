# Escaping Spoon Wheel Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`

Suggested art format: `art: ghosted-motion`

## Purpose

Turn a falling ball or pin into one indexed step of rotation using spoon bowls as paddle cups.

## Subject Brief

Show a small vertical wheel with several spoon bowls mounted around its rim. The wheel is on a central pivot. A stop or pawl prevents it from spinning freely.

A ball or falling pin lands in one spoon bowl, making the wheel rotate one station. As the wheel moves, the next spoon bowl comes into the catching position. A stop catches the wheel there. The ball exits or is captured before the next cycle.

The image should show the spoon bowls doing mechanical work: catching mass, creating torque, then indexing to a stop.

## Must Show

- Central wheel pivot.
- Spoon bowls arranged around the rim.
- Incoming ball or pin landing in one bowl.
- Stop/pawl that limits motion to one station.
- Exit or capture for the ball.

## Avoid

- A decorative spoon wreath.
- Free-spinning wheel with no indexing.
- Spoon bowls too shallow to catch anything.

## Open Questions

- Does the ball stay in the spoon or exit after indexing?
- Is the stop a ratchet pawl, detent, or physical bumper?
- How much mass is needed to turn the wheel reliably?
