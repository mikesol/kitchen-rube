# Domino-To-Cable Comb Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`

Suggested art format: `art: single-frame`

## Purpose

Convert one falling-tab event into several small cord tugs, allowing one sequence to trip multiple gates or indicators.

## Subject Brief

Show a row of falling tabs or domino-like paddles ending at a horizontal comb. The falling row does not pull the cords one by one. Instead, the final falling tab knocks a sliding comb or rake sideways.

The comb has several teeth. Each tooth holds a small loop of cord. When the comb slides, all the cord loops are tugged a short distance together. Each cord can lead to a different small gate, flag, or latch.

This should look like a mechanical translator: falling motion becomes one sideways sliding motion, and that sliding motion becomes several tension outputs.

## Must Show

- Final falling tab striking the comb.
- Comb or rake constrained to slide sideways.
- Multiple cord loops captured by comb teeth.
- Cords leading to separate outputs.

## Avoid

- Cords attached directly to every domino.
- Loose, unexplained string clutter.
- A comb that is not constrained to a clear linear motion.

## Open Questions

- How many cord outputs are useful before friction becomes silly?
- Should the comb tug all cords together or stagger them by tooth spacing?
- Does this need a spring return for reset?
