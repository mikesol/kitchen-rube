# Accordion Dominoes Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`, `clarity: needs-storyboard`

Suggested art format: `art: three-frame storyboard`

## Purpose

Convert one local push into a wide zigzag sequence across the wall, ending in a small mechanical output such as pulling a pin or opening a marble gate.

## Subject Brief

Show a shallow wall-mounted row of individual wooden paddles. Each paddle is like a thick domino tab mounted on its own pivot near the lower third of the tab. The paddles are not free-standing on a table; they are hinged to a horizontal backer board on the wall.

The paddles alternate their armed lean: first leans slightly left, second slightly right, third left, fourth right. The first released paddle rotates down and sideways under gravity. Its upper edge hits the next paddle from the side, not straight-on. The next paddle rotates the opposite way and hits the next one. The chain travels as a left-right-left-right zigzag.

Frame 1 should show all paddles cocked and waiting. Frame 2 should show the first two or three paddles mid-transfer with ghosted motion arcs and visible contact points. Frame 3 should show the last paddle pulling a small pin, lifting a latch, or opening a marble gate.

## Must Show

- Separate pivots for each paddle.
- Alternating left/right lean in the armed state.
- Side contact from one paddle into the next.
- The zigzag transfer path.
- A final output that does real work.

## Avoid

- A normal straight domino row.
- A single shared axle that makes all paddles move together.
- Arrows that imply motion the geometry cannot perform.
- Decorative paddles that do not contact each other.

## Open Questions

- Are pivots near the bottom, center, or side of each paddle?
- Does each paddle need a stop so it does not over-rotate?
- Is the final output better as a pin pull, marble gate, or cord tug?
