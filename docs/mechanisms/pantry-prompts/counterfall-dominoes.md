# Counterfall Dominoes Prompt Brief

Source: [Mechanism Pantry](../pantry.md)

Clarity: `clarity: expanded`

Suggested art format: `art: ghosted-motion`

## Purpose

Make a sequence appear to climb upward while staying mechanically honest: each higher tab already stores its own energy, and the lower event only releases it.

## Subject Brief

Show a staggered diagonal row of wall-mounted tabs rising from lower left to upper right. Each tab is cocked and held by a small latch, spring, counterweight, or over-center balance. A lower tab falls first and trips the latch of the next higher tab.

The next higher tab then falls because it was already armed, not because the lower tab pushed it uphill. The visual effect is a rising sequence of falling actions: lower tab releases higher tab, higher tab releases the next higher tab.

The concept should make the stored-energy trick visible enough that it does not look like impossible gravity.

## Must Show

- Each upper tab has its own latch or stored-energy state.
- Lower tab releases, not pushes, the next higher tab.
- Diagonal upward progression.
- Stops or guards so armed tabs cannot fly loose.

## Avoid

- A ball or tab literally rolling uphill without an energy source.
- Hidden magic springs.
- A sequence where the lower tab physically cannot reach the higher latch.

## Open Questions

- Are tabs counterweighted, spring-loaded, or over-center balanced?
- How is false release prevented during reset?
- Is this visually worth the added arming complexity?
