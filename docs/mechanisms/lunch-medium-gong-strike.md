# Mechanism: Lunch Medium Gong Strike

## Status

Dream

## Purpose

Transform the lunch route signal into a medium-pitched gong strike with a balanced, visible motion.

This dossier reserves the middle gong court role and sketches a pendulum-style candidate.

## Cultural Or Object Language

Lunch should feel balanced and social rather than frantic or heavy. A pendulum beater reads as measured, legible, and centrally weighted.

## Input

- Physical input: latch trip, falling tab, cable pull, or routed ball from the field.
- Energy input: low-energy release signal.
- Trigger condition: lunch path releases the pendulum beater.

## Output

- Physical output: pendulum beater swings into the medium gong once.
- Energy output: acoustic strike energy at the gong.
- Signal or route output: lunch run complete, with pendulum captured or damped after strike.

## Energy Source

Local pendulum potential energy, hand-armed before the run. A small spring or counterweight may assist if needed.

## Reset Or Arming Action

- Cock or lift the pendulum into its start position.
- Set the latch.
- Confirm rebound damping or catch is ready.

## Materials And Sourcing

- 3D printed: latch, pendulum hub, adjustable stop, beater mount.
- Bought: medium tuned gong, pivot hardware, bearings or bushings, felt/rubber bumper.
- Scavenged/found: kitchen utensil handle, small weight, old clock or metronome parts.
- Shop-made: gong bracket, pendulum arm, catch.

## Mounting And Depth

- Approximate footprint: center-right gong court, around `72-83 in` from left and `32-45 in` down from top.
- Approximate projection: `3-6 in`.
- Mounting assumptions: pendulum needs a clear swing arc and a hard stop or catch that prevents repeat strikes.

## Risks

- Force: pendulum must have enough travel for sound without overdriving the gong.
- Friction: pivot friction may make the strike unreliable.
- Tolerance: latch release angle will matter.
- Timing: the beater may bounce and double-strike.
- Safety: swing arc must not cross the outlet zone or protrude into a person path.
- Reset complexity: cocking should be visible and satisfying, not fragile.

## Tuning Notes

- Test beater material and pendulum length with the actual gong.
- Determine whether the pendulum should be damped, caught, or allowed to settle.
