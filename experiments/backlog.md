# Experiments Backlog

Open design questions awaiting proof-of-concept work. These are not full experiment writeups; they are the questions that must be answered with physical tests before the corresponding mechanism can be promoted into the wall. Move a question to its own dated experiment file when work starts.

## Trigger And Diverter

- Compare early split (three independent physical starts) vs shared launch with a diverter. Which gives clearer route identity and easier reset?
- Test simultaneous and partial meal selection behavior on candidate trigger hardware. A selector interlock is in the pantry; does it actually feel right in the hand?

## Breakfast High-Gong Strike

- Compare direct falling-ball strike vs rebound strike against the actual high gong. Which produces the brighter, more breakfast-coded sound? Which is more reliable to reset?
- Ball size, drop height, and contact-point sensitivity once candidate gongs exist.

## Lunch Medium-Gong Strike

- Test pendulum length and beater material against the actual medium gong.
- Decide whether the pendulum should be damped, caught, or allowed to settle naturally after the strike. A double-tap blocker is in the pantry; which option fits the lunch identity best?

## Dinner Low-Gong Strike

- Test heavy strikers off the wall before integrating. Start with low mass and small drop height.
- Quantify the safety envelope of a gravity-hammer-class strike: peak speed, rebound, audible decay.

## Stored Energy In General

- Find the lowest energy that produces an acceptable gong sound. Escalation should be evidence-driven, not assumed.
